  group_by(trans, trap_number)%>%
  summarise(
    count = sum(tl >= 450)

  mutate(across(everything(), ~replace_na(.x,0)))

Here are my initial thoughts.

CPUE for shallow depths and deep depths are kind of similar in 1991 and 1990. CPUE for deep depths are also kind of similar in 1991 and 1990. However, CPUE of shallow depths and deep depths, in both years, are not similar, and while I haven’t done statistics, I also am unsure if CPUE for shallow depths is similar to total CPUE in both years. All of these statements depend on what our criterion of “similar” is.
